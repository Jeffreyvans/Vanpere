How it works

Why VanPere

The best frames are the ones nobody staged — the ones your guests caught.

A wedding, a funeral, a graduation: every person in the room is holding a camera, and the photographs end up scattered across a dozen phones. VanPere Digital pulls them back into one quiet, well-kept gallery while the day is still happening.

Share the QR code on a table card, in a message, on the order of service. Guests scan, choose their best shots, and watch the gallery fill up.

FAQ

Questions, answered

Do guests need an account?

No. They scan the code or tap the link, upload their photographs and browse the gallery. There is nothing to register and no app to install.

Can I create my own organiser account?

VanPere Digital is administered by the operator. Ask them to create an event and send you the guest link.

Is location data removed from photos?

Yes. GPS coordinates and other identifying metadata are stripped from every shared version of an upload.

How long are photos kept?

Until the event expiry date you choose, then they are purged from storage. Download everything before then.

What happens with a bad connection?

Uploads are compressed in the browser and queued on the device. The queue syncs automatically as soon as the signal comes back.
