FAQ

Do guests need an account?

No. They scan, upload and view.

Can I create my own organiser account?

No. VanPere Digital is administered by the operator. Ask them to create an event and send you the guest link.

Is location data removed from photos?

Yes. GPS and other metadata are stripped from every shared version.

How long are photos kept?

Until the event expiry date you choose, then they are purged.
